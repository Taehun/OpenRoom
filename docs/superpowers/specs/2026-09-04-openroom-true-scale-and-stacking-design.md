# OpenRoom True-Scale Projection and Lamp Stacking Design

Date: 2026-09-04. Owner requests: (1) the mock room and furniture must be
drawn at their real relative sizes, and floor furniture must never be placed
off the floor; (2) stand lamps must be placeable on top of tables.

## 1. Outcome

Every cutout on the photo stage is sized from the object's real width and the
calibrated floor width at its depth, so a 2.1 m sofa is 2.1/6 of the floor's
width where it stands and a 0.45 m side table is 0.45/6 of it — no per-category
multipliers or clamps. Objects can only stand where the floor is: every
accepted position keeps the whole footprint inside the room, pointer drags land
on the floor trapezoid, and nothing can be dropped onto a wall. A floor lamp
whose base lies within a table's top is supported by that table: it rises by
the table's height, renders above it, and does not collide with it.

## 2. Invariants

- Scene stays metres; `rotation[1]` the only orientation; the Core 6 unchanged
  (`move_object` semantics unchanged: a lamp moved onto a table is accepted and
  reported with its supporting object).
- Projection stays pure and deterministic; the rug homography stays.
- Room dimensions in the mock (6.0 × 4.8 m, height 2.5 m) and the catalog's
  `dimensionsCm` are real-world plausible and are not changed by this work
  unless a value is physically wrong (a reviewer lists any).

## 3. True-scale projection (spec of `photo-projection.ts`)

- `floorWidthAt(depth)` = `floorRight(depth) − floorLeft(depth)` from the
  calibration (0.52 at the back wall, 0.92 at the front).
- `objectVisualWidth(object, room)` = the projected lateral extent of the
  object's oriented footprint: project the footprint corners with
  `projectRoomPoint`, take `max.x − min.x` in stage units, times 100. For an
  axis-aligned object this equals `widthM / room.width × floorWidthAt(depth)`.
  The cutout keeps its intrinsic aspect ratio, so height follows.
- `VISUAL_WIDTH_BOUNDS` is deleted; a single global sanity floor of 1.5 % keeps
  a fallback label readable.
- Vertical placement: the cutout's registered anchor sits on the projected
  floor point of the object's centre (unchanged). Supported objects (section 5)
  add a vertical offset of `supportHeightM × verticalScaleAt(depth)`, where
  `verticalScaleAt(depth)` = stage units per metre of height at that depth,
  derived as `floorWidthAt(depth) / room.width` (same as horizontal; the photo's
  perspective is approximated as uniform at a depth).
- Contact shadows keep using the projected footprint (already true-scale).

## 4. Floor-only placement

- `unprojectStagePoint` already clamps to the floor trapezoid; the command layer
  keeps footprints inside the room with the 0.1 m inset. Add a hard rule in
  `applySceneCommand` for `move`: after clamping, if any footprint corner would
  lie outside the room, the move is clamped along the offending axis (not
  rejected), so a drag never leaves an object hanging on a wall.
- The photo stage's drag preview uses the same clamp so the ghost never leaves
  the floor.

## 5. Lamp stacking

- Support relation: a `floor_lamp` is *supported* by a `coffee_table` or
  `side_table` when the lamp's XZ centre lies inside the table's oriented
  footprint. `supportOf(scene, lamp)` returns the table or null.
- Effects: `position[1]` of a supported lamp = table height + lamp height / 2
  (the store recomputes Y on every move/replace); collision checks between the
  lamp and its supporter are skipped; the lamp is layered above the table
  (zIndex + 1 stride); the inspector shows `On: <table title>`.
- `move_object` result and `get_scene` objects gain `supportedBy: objectId |
  null` (derived, not stored; JSON schema and Zod output schema updated; evals
  gain `lamp-on-side-table`).
- Solver (library only, no UI): a lamp gains candidates at the centre of each
  side table; supported lamps skip the collision hard constraint with their
  table. Not scored differently.
- The catalog gains three short table-height stand lamps (`floor_lamp`
  category, 45–60 cm tall) generated by the pipeline, so a lamp on a side table
  looks right; the existing 150–162 cm floor lamps may also be placed on a
  table (the physics allow it, the picture will look tall).

## 6. Tests

Unit: `objectVisualWidth` equals `widthM / room.width × floorWidth` for
axis-aligned objects at back, middle, front; rotated footprint extent; vertical
offset per depth; move clamping keeps every corner inside; `supportOf` inside /
edge / outside; Y recomputation on move and replace; layering; tool output
`supportedBy`. Component: a supported lamp's frame `top` is raised by the
projected table height. E2E: place the lamp on the side table through
`move_object` and assert `supportedBy`; drag a sofa toward the back wall and
assert its footprint stays inside the room.

## 7. Acceptance

1. The seed and redesigned rooms read at true relative scale (screenshot
   reviewed): sofa about 35 % of the floor width at mid-depth, side table about
   7 %, bookshelf about 15 %.
2. No drag or tool move leaves any footprint corner outside the room.
3. A lamp moved onto a side table rises onto it and is reported `supportedBy`.
