# Returns

<p><strong>Thirty days, for a full refund.</strong> The piece needs to be
unused and back in its original packaging.</p>

<p>Email us with your order number and we will send a returns label and book
the collection. Refunds reach the original payment method within five working
days of the piece arriving back with us.</p>

<p>Return shipping is free if the piece arrived damaged, faulty, or is not what
you ordered. Otherwise it is $29 for flat-packed items and $79 for anything
that needed a two-person delivery, deducted from the refund.</p>

<p>Plants are the exception: living stock cannot be returned once it has left
us, but tell us within seven days if one arrives in poor condition and we will
replace or refund it.</p>

<p><em>This is a demonstration store. Orders placed here are not fulfilled and
no payment is taken.</em></p>
