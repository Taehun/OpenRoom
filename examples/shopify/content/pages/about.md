# About

<p>OpenRoom is a furniture shop with a room planner attached, built to show what
a store looks like when an AI assistant can actually use it.</p>

<p>Describe the room you want. The assistant reads this catalog, places the
furniture where it fits, and hands the finished arrangement back as a cart —
the whole room, not one product at a time. Nothing is hidden behind a chat
window you cannot leave: every piece it picks has an ordinary product page, an
ordinary price, and an ordinary checkout.</p>

<p>Forty-three pieces across eight categories, each listed at its real measured
width, depth, and height in centimetres. That matters more than it sounds. A
planner that works from approximate sizes will happily put a two-metre sofa in
front of a doorway; one that works from measured sizes will not. What clears
the walkway on screen clears it in the room.</p>

<p>The store runs on Shopify with no server of its own and no access token: the
planner builds a normal cart permalink and points assistants at this store's
public commerce endpoint. It is a demonstration store, so orders here are not
fulfilled.</p>
