# Privacy

<p>OpenRoom is a demonstration store. It exists to show how a shop behaves when
an AI assistant can browse and buy from it, and it collects as little as that
demonstration needs.</p>

<p><strong>What the shop stores.</strong> This storefront runs on Shopify.
Shopify records the usual shop things — pages you visited, what went into a
cart, and, if you check out, the name and address you typed. Shopify's own
privacy notice governs that data.</p>

<p><strong>What the room planner stores.</strong> Nothing. The planner runs
entirely in your browser: the room you arrange never leaves the page, is not
sent to a server, and is not saved to an account. Adding a room to the cart
sends only the product identifiers and quantities, exactly as clicking "add to
cart" on a product page would.</p>

<p><strong>What we do not do.</strong> We do not sell personal data, we do not
build advertising profiles, and we do not pass anything to an AI provider — the
assistant that drives the planner is whichever one you brought, running on your
side of the connection.</p>

<p>Ask us to delete anything the store holds about you and we will, by email.</p>
