# Shipping

<p><strong>Free shipping on orders over $200.</strong> Below that, a flat $19
anywhere we deliver.</p>

<p>Orders leave the warehouse within two working days. Delivery takes a further
five to eight working days for most pieces; sofas, bookshelves, and anything
else that needs two people to carry are booked in with you by phone and arrive
inside two to three weeks.</p>

<p>Everything ships flat-packed except sofas and chairs, which arrive
assembled. Assembly instructions and the tools you need are in the box.</p>

<p>Measure your doorways, stairwells, and lift before you order. Every product
page carries the piece's packed dimensions as well as its assembled ones, and
we cannot refund a delivery that could not physically enter the building.</p>

<p><em>This is a demonstration store. Orders placed here are not fulfilled and
no payment is taken.</em></p>
