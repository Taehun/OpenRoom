# Shipping

<p>Nothing ships. OpenRoom is a demonstration store built to show how a shop
behaves when an AI assistant can browse and buy from it, and no order placed
here is fulfilled.</p>

<p>The catalog is real in one respect only: every piece carries its true
measured width, depth, and height in centimetres, because the room planner
needs honest dimensions to place furniture that fits. Prices, stock, and
delivery are not real.</p>
